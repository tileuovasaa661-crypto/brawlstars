About Brawl Stars

Brawl Stars is a free-to-play mobile game developed and published by Supercell, a company from Finland. Players choose a brawler with unique attacks and team up in short 3v3 matches or fight for survival in Showdown. The game mixes fast shooter action with team tactics and is available on iOS and Android. It started as a small test in Canada in 2017 and was released worldwide at the end of 2018.

Many ways to play

The game has many modes, so every match feels different. In Showdown you fight to be the last one standing: Solo Showdown has 10 players, Duo Showdown has teams of two, and Trio Showdown has teams of three. In 3v3 modes two teams compete for a goal.

  • Gem Grab: collect gems and hold 10 of them until the countdown ends.
  • Knockout: win a best-of-three contest, and defeated brawlers stay out until the next round.
  • Brawl Ball: score goals with a ball, like football.
  • Heist: destroy the enemy safe before they destroy yours.
  • Hot Zone: stay in the zones on the map for a combined 100 seconds.
  • Bounty: defeat opponents to earn stars.

Some events also offer 5v5 versions of modes, as well as special modes like Brawl Hockey and boss fights. The list of modes on the map changes over time.

Key dates

  1. 14 June 2017: Supercell announces Brawl Stars in a livestream.
  2. 15 June 2017: the game starts a soft launch in Canada on iOS.
  3. 19 January 2018: the soft launch expands to more countries, such as Finland and Sweden.
  4. 12 December 2018: worldwide release on iOS and Android.

Quick facts

  • Developer and publisher: Supercell.
  • Genre: a mix of MOBA and hero shooter.
  • Platforms: iOS and Android.
  • During the soft launch the game changed from portrait mode to landscape mode with joysticks.
  • In its first month after the worldwide release, the game earned more than 63 million US dollars.
