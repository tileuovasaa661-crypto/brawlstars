Brawl Stars: Fast 3v3 Battles on Your Phone

Welcome! This is a fan project about Brawl Stars, the mobile game by Supercell.

What is Brawl Stars?

Brawl Stars is a fast multiplayer game where every match takes only a few minutes. You choose a brawler and fight together with your team.

  • Play short matches in different game modes.
  • Unlock brawlers with unique attacks.
  • Team up with friends.
  • Collect skins.

How to start playing

  1. Download the game from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Finish the short tutorial.
  3. Try your first matches with Shelly.
  4. Test each game mode.
  5. Invite a friend.
